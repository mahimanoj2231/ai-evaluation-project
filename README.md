# ai-evaluation-project

## What I did
I tested AI answers for 10 questions and evaluated them for accuracy, clarity, and usefulness.

## How I evaluated
1. Asked same 10 questions to 2 AI tools
2. Checked if answer is correct, clear, and helpful
3. Gave score 1 to 5 for each answer
4. Wrote reason for each score

## Questions I used
1. What is the capital of Tamil Nadu?
2. How to calculate sum in Excel?
3. Write a formal email for job application
4. Explain photosynthesis in simple words
5. Translate "I am looking for a job" in Tamil
6. What is the difference between AI and Machine Learning?
7. Give 3 tips for interview preparation
8. Write a 2-line story about Chennai rain
9. What is 15% of 200? Explain step by step
10. Is Chennai a good city for freshers to find jobs? Why?

## Tools Used
- Meta AI, ChatGPT
- Excel / Google Sheets
- GitHub

## What I learned
How to check AI output quality and find mistakes in AI answers.
